What is a Phishing Link?

A phishing link is designed by attackers to look like a legitimate link (like the one you clicked on my story) while harvesting credentials (your passwords and account are now mine! hehe jk😈) or distributing malicious payloads (nasty viruses that will make your device run wonky-tonky). So now you understood what is a phishing link, let us understand,

Why to Avoid Unknown Links?

Clicking random links can trigger drive-by malware downloads (Malware that is downloaded without your knowledge.), credential theft (sites that impersonate legit websites to steal your info, yes YOUR ACCOUNT INFO!), or browser session compromise(its a bit advanced and unlikely to happen but still good to have awareness.).

💡 Quick Defense Tip

Always hover before you click to preview the actual destination URL. Look out for slight misspellings (typosquatting), mismatched domains, or unusual top-level domains (e.g., .xyz, .top) before entering any sensitive details.
