What is a Phishing Link?

A phishing link is a deceptive hyperlink (link that you can click on, the bluish text) crafted by malicious actors to masquerade (imposter, fake) as a legitimate website (such as your bank, email provider, or streaming service). These links are typically sent via spoofed emails, social media messages/stories containing links 😈, or suspicious text messages (smishing) designed to manipulate human trust.

Why You Shouldn't Click Random or Unknown Links

Clicking an unverified link bypasses (skips) many basic defenses, triggering risks such as:

  • Credential Harvesting: Fake login portals capture usernames, passwords, and 2FA tokens directly and your account is now gone! Like my other 3 accounts 🥲
  • Drive-by Downloads: Visiting an exploited web page can silently execute scripts to install ransomware, spyware, or keyloggers and now I could take away your passwords, bank details and many more sensitive information.
  • Session Hijacking: Malicious scripts can steal valid authentication cookies, granting attackers direct account access although this is a bit advanced and its unlikely it would happen but still its good to be aware.

💡 Quick Defense Tips from yours truly

Always hover before you click to preview the actual destination URL. Check for homoglyph attacks (letters that look same but are not), mismatched domains (speeling mistoks in the webcites), or unusual top-level domains (e.g., .xyz, .top) before interacting.
